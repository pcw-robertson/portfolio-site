I'm a product design leader with 16 years of experience building customer-facing products, from 0 to 1 launches to growth.

I work on products that depend on people coming back. I find what a customer returns for, design the experience around it, and ship it with engineering, then measure whether it's working: first use, repeat use, and what that does for the business.

At Condé Nast I lead a team of five designers across The New Yorker, Wired, Vanity Fair and Bon Appétit, designing for audiences of millions. Recent work includes the Wired app, two New Yorker games, a rebuilt archive and search, and the repositioning of Bon Appétit around recipes.\
I'm hands-on about quality and build design systems and AI-enabled workflows that bring design and engineering closer together.

Earlier, I led design at The Telegraph and The Times, and I studied Information Design at the London College of Communication.
