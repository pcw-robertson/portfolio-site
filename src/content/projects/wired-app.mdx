    <h2>The Opportunity</h2>
    <p>
      Through 2025, Wired's pointed journalism and fearless reporting drove a
      significant wave of new subscribers. The app was conceived as a way to
      engage these readers more deeply before renewal, building a direct,
      owned relationship with the audience to build lasting retention.
    </p>

    <h2>Role</h2>
    <p>
      Led strategy and execution end-to-end, from initial product thesis
      through design, prioritization, and launch, defining the core product
      experience to make the best place to experience Wired.
    </p>

    <p>
      The goal was to create the place to read, listen and watch Wired.
      Creating experiences native to the formats of text, audio and video,

<div class="grid">
  <div class="col-6">
    <h2>Takeaways</h2>
    <p>
      <strong>Speed with a small team</strong> — Beta in 2 months, shipped
      in 5. With a team of 2 designers forcing ruthless prioritisation on
      what actually mattered to the experience.
    </p>
    <p>
      <strong>Vision-led craft</strong> — we defined the guiding direction
      and saw it through to visual execution, in keeping with Wired's
      storied visual identity.
    </p>
    <p>
      <strong>Habit by design</strong> — every core decision, from tailored
      story packages to following mechanics, built around daily engagement,
      not just content delivery.
    </p>
