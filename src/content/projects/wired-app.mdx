    <h2>The Opportunity</h2>
    <p>Turn a surge of new subscribers into lasting retention. Through 2025, Wired's new editorial direction drove a significant wave of new subscribers.     <h2>Role</h2>
    <p>Led strategy and execution end to end, from the initial product thesis through design, prioritization, and launch, setting the vision and carrying it through to visual execution.</p>
    <h2>Outcome</h2>
    <ul class="outcome-list">
      <li>Beta in two months</li>
      <li>Launch in five months</li>
      <li>Early signs: 5x the time spent in the app vs web</li>
